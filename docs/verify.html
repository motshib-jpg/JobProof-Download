What is verified?

The QR contains a privacy-minimized snapshot signed by an ECDSA P-256 key stored in the Android device keystore. This page checks that digital signature and shows whether the client seal matched the job fingerprint when the QR was created. Customer name and phone number are not included. Device identity/account registration is a later server-backed trust layer.
